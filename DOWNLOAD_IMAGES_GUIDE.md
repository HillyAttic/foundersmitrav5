## Download Instructions

### For Undraw.co (Easiest - No Attribution Required):
1. Visit https://undraw.co/search
2. Type your search term
3. Click on the illustration you like
4. Click "Download SVG" or "Download PNG"
5. Choose your preferred color (you can customize!)
6. Save to `D:\foundersmitra\images\services\`

### For Freepik.com:
1. Visit the provided link
2. Browse free vectors (filter by "Free")
3. Click on the illustration
4. Click "Download" (may require free account)
5. Choose format: JPG or SVG
6. **Important:** If using free version, you must attribute Freepik
7. Save to `D:\foundersmitra\images\services\`

### For Vecteezy.com:
1. Visit the provided link
2. Filter by "Free"
3. Click on illustration
4. Click "Free Download"
5. May require free account
6. Save to `D:\foundersmitra\images\services\`
